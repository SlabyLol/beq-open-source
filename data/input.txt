Once upon a time there was a little language model called Beq.
Beq was born from pure PyTorch code and a lot of curiosity.
Beq learned to talk by reading many stories and conversations.
Beq does not use any external AI API. Everything runs locally.
People can chat with Beq through a simple web page.
Beq wants to become better every day by learning from more text.
The creators of Beq believe that open source AI should be transparent.
Anyone can look at the code, train their own version, and improve it.
This is the beginning of Beq, a small but real language model.
